# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, caste, region, native language, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, socioeconomic status, or religion.

## Our Standards
- Being respectful of aspirants from every region and linguistic background across India.
- Constructively reviewing question explanations and answer key disputes with citations from official keys or standard textbooks (NCERTs, official ministries).
- Zero tolerance for spam, coaching advertisements, predatory upsells, or pirated commercial test series.
